# Data desk notes

## Removing a person's exported records

After a verified removal request, erase the person's downloadable archive and
the temporary export token. Keep only the request identifier and completion
time in the compliance ledger.

## Helping someone retrieve an archive

Support may resend a download link after confirming the account owner. The link
expires after one hour; do not attach the archive to an email.

## Scope

This guide does not change retention settings for shared team material.
