# On-call notebook

## Asking for help during an outage

Page the incident lead when two independent checks show the service is failing.
If no lead responds within ten minutes, notify the duty manager and add the
timeline to the incident channel.

## Handover after recovery

Before ending a shift, write the unresolved risk, the owner of the next action,
and the time of the next check in the handover note.

## Routine noise

One isolated failed health probe is recorded but does not page anyone.
