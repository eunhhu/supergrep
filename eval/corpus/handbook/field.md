# Field guide

## Reversing a rollout

When a release causes a customer-facing regression, point the traffic switch at
the previously tagged image. Record the incident identifier before changing the
switch, and do not delete the failed image until the follow-up is complete.

## Reviewing a proposal

Describe the behavior that concerns you, cite the smallest example, and suggest
a next step. Avoid labels about the author; the review should make the change
easier to improve.

## Small vocabulary note

The examples may contain résumé, 한글, and 🧭 so offset handling is visible.
